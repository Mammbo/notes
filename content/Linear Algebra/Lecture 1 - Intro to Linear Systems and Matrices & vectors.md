# Section 1.2  Matrices & Vectors

- we use matrix notation to forget all about  the variables etc and focus entierly on the numbers

transform this system of linear equations to 
$$ 
\begin{cases} 
x + 2y - 3z = 14 \\
3x - y - z = -2 \\
x - 2y + z = 2 
\end{cases} 
$$
transform this system of linear equations to -->
$$ 
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 & 14 \\ 
3 & -1 & -1 & -2 \\ 
1 & -2 & 1 & 2 
\end{bmatrix} 
$$

**`Def:A n x m matrix is simply a collection of n * m numbers in n rows and m columns.`**
