## Lecture Schedule (tentative)

All textbook readings are **optional**; if you learn well from a textbook, we suggest one of these:

- Newstead, _An Infinite Descent into Pure Mathematics_ — free online [here](https://infinitedescent.org/).
- Scheinerman, _Mathematics: A Discrete Introduction_.
- Rosen, _Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications_.
