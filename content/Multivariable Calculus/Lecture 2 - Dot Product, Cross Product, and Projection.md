## The Dot Product
- Dot product only cares about relative position not absolute position.

 $$v * w = \sum_{i=1}^nv_iw_i$$
 ex: 
 $$\begin{bmatrix}4 \\2\\-1 \end{bmatrix} * \begin{bmatrix}-2 \\6\\0 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{matrix} -8 \\ +12\end{matrix} = 4 $$
